## Acceptance observations

- Non-root, read-only application containers passed health-gated Compose
  startup and the complete v2 HTTP/TCP/metrics/TTL protocol surface.
- Pausing Redis kept liveness and established ECHO traffic available, made
  readiness fail and new AUTH fail closed, then recovered readiness, AUTH,
  reports, and the retained config version after unpause.
- The deliberately constrained one-worker/64-slot benchmark exposed seven
  Response Queue rejections and 26 request failures at 500 clients with a
  4096-byte payload. The four-worker/4096-slot comparison completed the same
  10,000 requests without rejection. The raw failure is retained as intended
  overload evidence rather than normalized away.
- Both old Kubernetes Gateway pods logged graceful shutdown start, completed
  their bounded drain, closed old listeners, and disappeared from endpoints.
  The reconnecting client remained below the 10-second outage bound.

## Limits and release decision

These are short shared-runner and single-node demonstrations, not production
capacity or availability claims. They do not prove TLS, multi-node failure,
Redis high availability or host-loss durability, zero-disconnect rolling
updates, or a production SLO. Absolute benchmark values must be reproduced on
target hardware before sizing.

All frozen v2 functional, sanitizer, fault, Docker/Redis, benchmark,
Kubernetes, documentation, and evidence gates pass. The retained failed Kind
preflight is documented under `results/kubernetes/runtime-ci-preflight/` and
its root causes are corrected in the passing rerun. The candidate is therefore
approved for merge, annotated tag `v2.0.0`, and GitHub Release publication.
