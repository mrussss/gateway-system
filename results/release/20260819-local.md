`scripts/release_gate.sh --full` or the manual Smoke/Kubernetes/Benchmark
workflows in a capable environment, commit their raw artifacts, rerun the link
check from a clean tree, and only then tag and publish.

local number is substituted for Redis/container/cluster evidence.
